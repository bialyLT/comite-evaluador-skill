# Output Format

## Round shape

Each round is relayed in this order.

### Ronda N — <objetivo de la ronda>

**⚖️ Jurado — preguntas de la ronda**

1. [pregunta dirigida al 🛡️ Defensor]
2. [pregunta dirigida al 🔍 Escéptico]
3. [pregunta dirigida al 📐 Metodólogo]

> 🛡️ **DEFENSOR:** [postura y evidencia citada]
>
> 🔍 **ESCÉPTICO:** [rebatida y la trampa concreta]
>
> 📐 **METODÓLOGO:** [integración, huecos pendientes y veredicto]
>
> `ITERAR: <hueco>` o `CERRAR: <razón>`

Keep every posture to a short paragraph. Cite section, page, line or quoted phrase.

## Final response table

Close the deliberation with this table. One row per evaluated axis, three to six rows.

| Eje evaluado | 🛡️ Defensor | 🔍 Escéptico | 📐 Metodólogo y resolución |
|---|---|---|---|

Then one line with the single immediate next action for the researcher.

## Audit table (Rosario Martínez mode)

When the material is a thesis, research report or project under formal evaluation, close with this compliance matrix instead of the response table. One row per audited piece, ordered by rule R1, R2, R3, then sections A, B, C, D.

| Pieza auditada | Exigido por | Encaja / No encaja / Falta | Evidencia | Acción correctiva |
|---|---|---|---|---|

State each non-fitting piece as a fit failure with its consequence, never as a mere suggestion. Close with the single immediate next action.

## Candidate research lines

When the user asks what investigations the corpus and the idea enable, close with this table. One row per candidate, three to six rows, ordered by strength of the gap.

| Línea de investigación | Vacío que ataca (con evidencia del corpus) | Método | Viabilidad | Recursos | Veredicto del comité |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Discard any candidate whose gap is not identified in the corpus. Close with the single immediate next action.

## Static dictamen variant

When the user asks for a written dictamen or informe, use these shapes instead of the round shape.

### PARTE 1 — Diagnóstico Individual

Four blockquote cards, in this order. The Jurado card holds 2 to 4 numbered questions.

> 🛡️ **DEFENSOR:** [strongest points, contribution potential, defense of the gap]
> 🔍 **ESCÉPTICO:** [weak assumptions, voids, concrete traps]
> 📐 **METODÓLOGO:** [variable coherence, measurable data, boundary, viability]
> ⚖️ **JURADO:** [hard defense questions]

### PARTE 2 — Debate Cruzado

A short dynamic dialogue on the single sharpest point.

- **⚖️ Jurado:** [opens with the most incisive question]
- **🛡️ Defensor:** [defends the value of the proposal]
- **🔍 Escéptico:** [rebuts and names the trap]
- **📐 Metodólogo:** [proposes the concrete resolution: variables, data, study design]

### PARTE 3 — Hoja de Ruta

| Lo que ya está sólido (Mantener) | Lo que debo corregir o delimitar (Ajustar) | La pregunta clave que debo responder ahora |
|---|---|---|

Close the whole response with the single next action for the researcher.
